---

## 📊 Key Metrics Calculated

- **Inventory Value**: Quantity × Selling Price
- **Cost Value**: Quantity × Cost Price
- **Profit Potential**: Quantity × (Selling Price - Cost)
- **Profit Margin**: (Profit / Selling Price) × 100%
- **Stock Health**: (Total Products - Low Stock) / Total Products × 100%
- **Category Performance**: Sum of values per category
- **Location Distribution**: Unit count per location
